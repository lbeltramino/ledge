# Third-party notices

## Caveat

`Resources/Fonts/Caveat.ttf` is the Caveat typeface by Pablo Impallari, Rodrigo
Fuenzalida and Dave Crossland, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The full licence text is in `Resources/Fonts/OFL.txt` and ships inside the app
bundle.

Caveat is the default face for note bodies. Nothing else in Ledge is third-party
code: the SQLite index talks to the system `libsqlite3`, the ZIP container in
`LedgeStore/Zip.swift` is written from scratch, and there are no package
dependencies.
